Solving Equations 1

Solve one- and multi-step linear equations by undoing operations in reverse order and checking the solution.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Solving Linear Equations

A solution makes the equation true

Solving means finding the value of the variable that makes both sides equal. You can check by substituting your answer back into the original equation.

If \(3x+4=19\), then \(x=5\) because \(3(5)+4=19\).

Keep the equation balanced

Whatever operation you perform on one side of an equation must also be performed on the other side.

Think of an equation as a balance scale: equal changes preserve equality.
New Learning

Undo Operations in Reverse Order

1

Simplify first when helpful.

Remove brackets using distribution and combine like terms before isolating x.

2

Undo addition or subtraction.

Move the constant term by applying the inverse operation to both sides.

3

Undo multiplication or division.

Divide or multiply both sides to leave the variable alone, then verify.

Worked ExampleTwo-step equation
\(3x+5=29\)
  1. Subtract 5 from both sides: 3x = 24.
  2. Divide both sides by 3: x = 8.
  3. Check: 3(8) + 5 = 29 ✓
Worked ExampleBrackets on one side
\(4(x-2)+3=19\)
  1. Distribute: 4x − 8 + 3 = 19.
  2. Combine constants: 4x − 5 = 19.
  3. Add 5, then divide by 4: 4x = 24 → x = 6.
