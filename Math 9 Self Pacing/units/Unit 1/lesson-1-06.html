Lesson 1.06

Four Operations Review

Review addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division with positive and negative numbers.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Four Operations

Addition and Subtraction

Use number-line or sign-rule thinking for signed sums and differences.

-8 - (-3) = -5

Subtracting a negative is equivalent to adding.

Multiplication and Division

Determine the sign first, then calculate the magnitude.

-42 ÷ -6 = 7

Same signs produce a positive quotient.

New Learning

Mixed Operations Review

1

Identify the operation.

Decide whether the problem asks for a sum, difference, product, or quotient.

2

Determine the sign.

For multiplication and division, use the sign rules before calculating.

3

Check reasonableness.

Estimate or reverse the operation to confirm your answer.

Worked ExampleMixed Review
-15 + 9
  1. Start at -15.
  2. Adding 9 moves 9 places right.
  3. -15 + 9 = -6.
Worked ExampleMixed Review
(-8)(7)
  1. The signs are different, so the answer is negative.
  2. 8 × 7 = 56.
  3. Therefore, (-8)(7) = -56.
