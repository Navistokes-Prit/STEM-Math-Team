Lesson 1.01

Operations and Multiplying Decimals

Review place value and rounding, then learn how to multiply decimals accurately and efficiently.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Place Value and Rounding

Place Value

A digit's value depends on its position in the number.

32.045

The digit 4 is in the hundredths place, so its value is 0.04.

Rounding

Use the digit immediately to the right of the place you are rounding.

27.467 → 27.5

27.467 rounded to the nearest tenth is 27.5.

New Learning

Multiplying Decimals

1

Multiply as whole numbers first.

Temporarily ignore the decimal points and calculate the product.

2

Count the decimal places.

Add the number of decimal places in both factors.

3

Place the decimal in the product.

The answer must have the same total number of decimal places.

Worked ExampleMental Multiplication
0.03 × 0.05
  1. Ignore the decimals: 3 × 5 = 15.
  2. There are 2 decimal places in 0.03 and 2 in 0.05.
  3. The product needs 4 decimal places.
  4. 0.03 × 0.05 = 0.0015.
Worked ExampleWritten Multiplication
15.2 × 0.14
  1. Ignore the decimals: 152 × 14 = 2128.
  2. 15.2 has 1 decimal place and 0.14 has 2.
  3. The product needs 3 decimal places.
  4. 15.2 × 0.14 = 2.128.
