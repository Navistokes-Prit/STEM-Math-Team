Lesson 1.02

Dividing Decimals

Use place value and division strategies to calculate quotients involving decimals.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Multiplication Review

Multiplication Connection

Division reverses multiplication, so multiplication facts can help you check a quotient.

6 × 8 = 48

Therefore, 48 ÷ 8 = 6.

Decimal Place Value

Multiplying or dividing by powers of ten changes place value.

4.8 ÷ 10 = 0.48

Each digit moves one place to the right when dividing by 10.

New Learning

Dividing by Decimals

1

Make the divisor a whole number.

If the divisor is a decimal, move its decimal point to the right until it becomes a whole number.

2

Move both decimal points equally.

Whatever change you make to the divisor must also be made to the dividend.

3

Divide using a familiar method.

Use long division or the bus-stop method, then check by multiplying.

Worked ExampleDecimal ÷ Whole Number
8.4 ÷ 4
  1. Divide 84 ÷ 4 = 21.
  2. Because 8.4 is ten times smaller than 84, the quotient is ten times smaller than 21.
  3. 8.4 ÷ 4 = 2.1.
Worked ExampleDecimal Divisor
6.72 ÷ 0.8
  1. Move both decimal points one place right: 67.2 ÷ 8.
  2. 67.2 ÷ 8 = 8.4.
  3. Therefore, 6.72 ÷ 0.8 = 8.4.
