Lesson 1.09

BEDMAS and Square Roots

Evaluate square and cube roots, estimate non-perfect square roots, and use roots within BEDMAS.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Squares, Cubes, and Roots

Perfect Squares

A perfect square is the result of multiplying an integer by itself.

8² = 64

Therefore, √64 = 8.

Perfect Cubes

A perfect cube is the result of multiplying an integer three times.

4³ = 64

Therefore, ∛64 = 4.

New Learning

Roots and Estimation

1

Square roots undo squaring.

√49 = 7 because 7² = 49.

2

Cube roots undo cubing.

∛125 = 5 because 5³ = 125.

3

Estimate non-perfect roots using nearby perfect squares.

Since 49 < 58 < 64, we know 7 < √58 < 8.

Worked ExampleExact Root
√144
  1. 12² = 144.
  2. Therefore, √144 = 12.
Worked ExampleEstimate a Root
√70
  1. 8² = 64 and 9² = 81.
  2. 64 < 70 < 81.
  3. Therefore, 8 < √70 < 9.
