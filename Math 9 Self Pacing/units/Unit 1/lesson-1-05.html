Lesson 1.05

Multiplying and Dividing Negatives

Determine the sign of products and quotients involving positive and negative integers.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Signed Number Review

Magnitude First

The sign and the size of a number can be considered separately.

-6 × 4

First calculate 6 × 4, then determine the sign.

Division as a Fraction

A division statement can also be written as a fraction.

-24 ÷ 6 = -24/6

Both forms represent the same quotient.

New Learning

Sign Rules for Multiplication and Division

1

Same signs give a positive result.

Positive × positive and negative × negative are positive.

2

Different signs give a negative result.

Positive × negative and negative × positive are negative.

3

The same rule works for division.

Same signs → positive; different signs → negative.

Worked ExampleMultiplication
(-7)(-5)
  1. The signs are the same, so the result is positive.
  2. 7 × 5 = 35.
  3. Therefore, (-7)(-5) = 35.
Worked ExampleDivision
-48 ÷ 6
  1. The signs are different, so the result is negative.
  2. 48 ÷ 6 = 8.
  3. Therefore, -48 ÷ 6 = -8.
