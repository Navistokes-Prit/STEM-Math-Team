Lesson 1.10

Comparing and Ordering Rational Numbers

Convert, simplify, compare, and order fractions, mixed numbers, and other rational values.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Types of Fractions

Proper and Improper Fractions

A proper fraction has a numerator smaller than its denominator. An improper fraction has a numerator larger than its denominator.

3/8 and 5/4

3/8 is proper; 5/4 is improper.

Mixed Fractions

A mixed fraction contains a whole number and a fraction.

1 2/3

This can also be written as the improper fraction 5/3.

New Learning

Convert, Simplify, Compare

1

Convert when helpful.

Improper fractions and mixed numbers can be converted between forms.

2

Simplify fractions.

Divide numerator and denominator by a common factor.

3

Use common denominators to compare.

When denominators match, compare the numerators.

Worked ExampleImproper to Mixed
14/3
  1. 14 ÷ 3 = 4 remainder 2.
  2. The whole number is 4 and the remainder becomes the new numerator.
  3. 14/3 = 4 2/3.
Worked ExampleCompare Fractions
3/4 and 5/8
  1. Use denominator 8.
  2. 3/4 = 6/8.
  3. 6/8 > 5/8, so 3/4 > 5/8.
