Review and Exponential Form

Write repeated multiplication using exponential notation and connect powers to square and cube numbers.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Repeated Multiplication

From Addition to Multiplication

Multiplication is a short way to represent repeated addition.

4 + 4 + 4 = 3 × 4

Repeated multiplication can also be written in a shorter form.

Base and Exponent

The base is the repeated factor. The exponent tells how many times it appears.

5⁴

5 is the base and 4 is the exponent.

New Learning

Exponential Form

1

Identify the repeated factor.

That repeated factor becomes the base.

2

Count how many factors appear.

The number of factors becomes the exponent.

3

Recognize squares and cubes.

An exponent of 2 means squared; an exponent of 3 means cubed.

Worked ExampleExpanded to Exponential
7 × 7 × 7 × 7
  1. The repeated factor is 7.
  2. There are 4 factors.
  3. The exponential form is 7⁴.
Worked ExampleEvaluate a Power
3⁴
  1. Write as repeated multiplication: 3 × 3 × 3 × 3.
  2. 3 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 81.
  3. Therefore, 3⁴ = 81.
