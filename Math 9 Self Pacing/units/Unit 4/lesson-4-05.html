Lesson 4.05

Similar Shapes

Recognize similar figures, calculate scale factors, and solve for unknown corresponding side lengths.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Similarity

Same Shape, Different Size

Two shapes are similar when one is an enlargement or reduction of the other.

same angles • proportional sides

The shapes do not need to be the same size.

Scale Factor

Every corresponding side changes by the same scale factor.

new side = original side × scale factor

Compare matching sides, not sides chosen only because they look alike.

New Learning

Solving Similar-Shape Problems

1

Identify a pair of corresponding sides.

Use a pair where both lengths are known to determine the scale factor.

2

Use the same scale factor for every side.

Multiplying or dividing by different factors means the figures are not similar.

3

Set up a proportion for missing lengths.

Keep corresponding sides in the same order.

Worked Example Scale Factor
A side changes from \(4\text{ cm}\) to \(10\text{ cm}\).
  1. Scale factor = 10 ÷ 4.
  2. Scale factor = 2.5.
Worked Example Missing Side
\(\frac{x}{12}=\frac{4}{6}\)
  1. The scale factor from 6 to 12 is 2.
  2. x = 4 × 2.
  3. x = 8.
