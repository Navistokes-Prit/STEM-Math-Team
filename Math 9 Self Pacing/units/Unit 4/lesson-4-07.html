Lesson 4.07

Area and Composite Shapes

Calculate areas of common 2D shapes and solve composite-shape problems by splitting or subtracting regions.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Area of 2D Shapes

Common Area Formulas

Rectangles, squares, triangles, and circles each use a specific area formula.

A = lw, A = s², A = ½bh, A = πr²

Area is always reported in square units.

Composite Shapes

A composite shape is made from two or more basic shapes.

add or subtract areas

Choose a split that turns the diagram into shapes with familiar area formulas.

New Learning

Splitting and Subtracting

1

Add areas when the pieces combine to make the whole shape.

Find each component area and add them.

2

Subtract areas when a piece has been removed.

Find the area of the large shape, then subtract the missing region.

3

For circles, use radius carefully.

If a diameter is given, divide by 2 before using A = πr².

Worked Example Cut-Off Corner
A 13 cm by 9 cm rectangle has a right triangle of base 4 cm and height 4 cm removed.
  1. Rectangle area = 13 × 9 = 117 cm².
  2. Triangle area = ½(4)(4) = 8 cm².
  3. Remaining area = 109 cm².
Worked Example Circle
Radius \(=5\text{ cm}\)
  1. A = πr².
  2. A = 25π.
  3. A ≈ 78.5 cm².
