Lesson 4.03

Transformations

Describe and perform translations, reflections, and rotations while tracking image notation.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Transformation Vocabulary

Translation

A translation slides a shape. The image keeps the same shape, size, and orientation.

right/left, then up/down

A translation can also be described by a vector.

Image Notation

Transformed points are marked with a prime symbol, such as A′, B′, and C′.

A → A′

The original is the object; the result is the image.

New Learning

Reflection and Rotation

1

A reflection flips a shape across a line of reflection.

Each image point is the same perpendicular distance from the mirror line as its original point.

2

A rotation turns a shape about a fixed centre.

A rotation must specify the centre, angle, and direction.

3

Common rotation angles are 90°, 180°, 270°, and 360°.

Clockwise and anticlockwise directions must be distinguished when the angle is not 180°.

Worked Example Translation
\((2,1)\) translated 5 right and 3 down
  1. Add 5 to the x-coordinate.
  2. Subtract 3 from the y-coordinate.
  3. Image point: (7, -2).
Worked Example Reflection
Reflect \((-4,3)\) in the y-axis.
  1. The y-coordinate stays the same.
  2. The x-coordinate changes sign.
  3. Image point: (4, 3).
