Lesson 4.08

Surface Area of Prisms

Use nets and face areas to calculate the total surface area of cuboids, triangular prisms, and composite prisms.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Nets and Prism Vocabulary

Prism

A prism has flat faces, two parallel polygon bases, and a uniform cross-section.

base • lateral faces • height

A prism is named for the shape of its uniform cross-section.

Surface Area

Surface area is the total area of every outside face of a 3D object.

SA = sum of face areas

A net helps make sure each face is counted exactly once.

New Learning

Surface Area Strategies

1

For a rectangular prism, count the three pairs of equal faces.

SA = 2lw + 2lh + 2wh.

2

For a triangular prism, find both triangular ends and all three rectangular lateral faces.

The lateral rectangles use the three side lengths of the triangular base.

3

For composite prism bases, find the cross-sectional area and perimeter carefully.

The middle surface area can be found from cross-section perimeter × prism length.

Worked Example Rectangular Prism
2 cm × 3 cm × 5 cm
  1. SA = 2(2×3) + 2(2×5) + 2(3×5).
  2. SA = 12 + 20 + 30.
  3. SA = 62 cm².
Worked Example Triangular Prism
Right triangle sides 6, 8, 10 cm; prism length 7.2 cm
  1. Two triangular ends: 2(½×6×8) = 48 cm².
  2. Lateral area = (6+8+10)(7.2) = 172.8 cm².
  3. Total SA = 220.8 cm².
