Lesson 4.04

Line and Rotational Symmetry

Identify lines of symmetry and determine the order of rotational symmetry for common polygons.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Symmetry Definitions

Line Symmetry

A line of symmetry divides a shape into two equal mirror-image parts.

mirror line

Folding along the line would make the two halves match.

Rotational Symmetry

A shape has rotational symmetry when it matches itself after a turn of less than or equal to 360°.

order = number of matches

Count how many times the shape fits exactly onto itself in one full turn.

New Learning

Symmetry of Common Quadrilaterals

1

A square has 4 lines of symmetry and rotational order 4.

It matches after quarter-turn rotations.

2

A rectangle has 2 lines of symmetry and rotational order 2.

It matches after a half-turn.

3

A general parallelogram has no lines of symmetry and rotational order 2.

Line symmetry and rotational symmetry are different properties.

Worked Example Square
How many lines of symmetry?
  1. Vertical, horizontal, and both diagonals work.
  2. There are 4 lines of symmetry.
Worked Example Parallelogram
What is the rotational order?
  1. A half-turn maps the shape onto itself.
  2. A full turn also matches.
  3. The rotational order is 2.
