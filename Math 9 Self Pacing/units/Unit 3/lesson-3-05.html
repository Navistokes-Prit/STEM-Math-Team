Lesson 3.05

Factoring Polynomials

Find greatest common factors and factor polynomial expressions by reversing distribution.

Practice LevelFoundation
Quick Review

Greatest Common Factor

Common Factors

A common factor divides every number or term. The greatest common factor is the largest factor shared by all terms.

30 and 36

Their greatest common factor is 6.

Factoring Reverses Expansion

To factor an expression, take the greatest common factor outside brackets.

6t + 9m

3(2t + 3m).

New Learning

Factoring with a Greatest Common Factor

1

Find the greatest common numerical factor.

List factors or use divisibility to find the largest coefficient that divides every term.

2

Take common variables using the smallest exponent.

If every term contains x, factor out x. If they contain x³ and x⁴, the common factor can include x³.

3

Divide each original term by the common factor.

The quotients become the terms inside the brackets.

Worked ExampleNumerical GCF
-6b2 - 8
  1. The greatest common numerical factor is 2.
  2. Taking out -2 makes the leading term inside positive.
  3. Answer: -2(3b² + 4).
Worked ExampleVariable GCF
9x4 - 8x3
  1. Both terms contain x³.
  2. Factor out x³.
  3. Answer: x³(9x - 8).
