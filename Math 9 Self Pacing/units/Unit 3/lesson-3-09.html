Lesson 3.09

Area and Volume with Variables

Apply area, surface-area, and volume ideas to algebraic dimensions, word problems, and unit conversions.

Practice LevelFoundation
Quick Review

Area and Volume Applications

Surface Area

Surface area is the total area of all outside faces of a three-dimensional object.

SA = sum of face areas

A rectangular prism has three pairs of matching faces.

Area Unit Conversion

When converting squared units, the linear conversion factor must also be squared.

1 m2 = 10,000 cm2

Because 1 m = 100 cm, square the factor 100.

New Learning

Geometry with Algebraic Dimensions

1

Write the formula before substituting or expanding.

This makes it clear which dimensions are being multiplied.

2

Treat algebraic dimensions like algebraic factors.

For example, a rectangle with sides x and x + 4 has area x(x + 4).

3

Keep units consistent.

Convert lengths, areas, or volumes before combining measurements that use different units.

Worked ExampleVariable Rectangle
l = x + 3,   w = 2x
  1. A = lw.
  2. A = (x + 3)(2x).
  3. A = 2x² + 6x.
Worked ExampleArea Conversion
16 m2 to cm2
  1. 1 m² = 10,000 cm².
  2. 16 × 10,000 = 160,000.
  3. Answer: 160,000 cm².
