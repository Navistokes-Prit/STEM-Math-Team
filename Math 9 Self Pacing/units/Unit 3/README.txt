MATHJAX / LIVE ANSWERS
----------------------
Generated math is typeset using MathJax.

Student answers render live as they type.

Examples:
3x^2+5x-4
3x^{2}+5x-4
x(x+4)
\frac{6x+12}{3}

The checker compares mathematical polynomial equivalence rather than raw
characters. Reordered equivalent polynomials are accepted.

Factoring questions additionally require factored form and the generated
greatest common factor.
