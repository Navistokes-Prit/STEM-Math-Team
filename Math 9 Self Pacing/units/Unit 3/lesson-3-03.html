Lesson 3.03

Distribution Property

Expand brackets by multiplying the outside factor by every term inside.

Practice LevelFoundation
Quick Review

Multiplying Algebraic Terms

Distributive Law

A number or variable outside brackets multiplies every term inside the brackets.

5(x + 3)

Multiply 5 by x and multiply 5 by 3.

Watch the Signs

A negative outside the brackets changes the sign of each term when distributed.

-4(x - 6)

-4(x) + (-4)(-6) = -4x + 24.

New Learning

Expanding Brackets

1

Multiply every term inside the brackets.

The arrow method can help show each multiplication.

2

Apply integer sign rules.

A negative times a negative is positive; a negative times a positive is negative.

3

Use exponent laws when variables multiply.

For example, x(x - 5) becomes x² - 5x.

Worked ExampleInteger Coefficient
4(-3a + 5)
  1. 4(-3a) = -12a.
  2. 4(5) = 20.
  3. Answer: -12a + 20.
Worked ExampleVariable Coefficient
x(2x + 3)
  1. x(2x) = 2x².
  2. x(3) = 3x.
  3. Answer: 2x² + 3x.
