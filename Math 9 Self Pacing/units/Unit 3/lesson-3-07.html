Lesson 3.07

Area

Review perimeter and calculate the area of common two-dimensional shapes using the correct formula and perpendicular height.

Practice LevelFoundation
Quick Review

Perimeter Review

Perimeter

Perimeter is the total distance around the outside of a shape.

P = side + side + side + ...

Add every outside edge exactly once.

Area

Area is the amount of space inside a two-dimensional object.

A = length × width

Different shapes use different area formulas.

New Learning

Area of Common Shapes

1

Use the correct formula for the shape.

Rectangles use length × width; triangles use one-half base × perpendicular height.

2

Use perpendicular height.

The height used in an area formula is the perpendicular distance from the base.

3

Write square units.

Area is measured in units such as cm², m², or mm².

Worked ExampleTriangle
b = 12 cm,   h = 5 cm
  1. A = ½bh.
  2. A = ½(12)(5).
  3. A = 30 cm².
Worked ExampleRectangle
l = 9 m,   w = 6 m
  1. A = lw.
  2. A = 9(6).
  3. A = 54 m².
