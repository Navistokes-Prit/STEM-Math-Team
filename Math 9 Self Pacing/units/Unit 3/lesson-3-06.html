Lesson 3.06

Equivalent Polynomials

Determine polynomial degree and decide whether differently written expressions are equivalent.

Practice LevelFoundation
Quick Review

Parts and Degree of a Polynomial

Degree of a Term

For a term containing more than one variable, add the variable exponents to find the term's degree.

2ab3

The term degree is 1 + 3 = 4.

Degree of a Polynomial

The degree of a polynomial is the greatest degree of any term in the polynomial.

7a3 - 1 + 2ab3

The term 2ab³ has degree 4, so the polynomial has degree 4.

New Learning

Recognizing Equivalent Polynomials

1

Equivalent polynomials have the same value for every allowed value of the variables.

They may look different because terms have been reordered or like terms have been combined.

2

Order does not change a sum.

Commutative and associative properties allow terms to be rearranged before collecting like terms.

3

Simplify both expressions to compare them.

If both simplify to the same polynomial, they are equivalent.

Worked ExampleReordered Terms
3x2 + 5x - 4   and   -4 + 5x + 3x2
  1. The terms are identical; only the order changed.
  2. Both simplify to 3x² + 5x - 4.
  3. The polynomials are equivalent.
Worked ExampleCombine First
2x + 4x + 3   and   6x + 3
  1. Collect like terms on the first expression.
  2. 2x + 4x + 3 = 6x + 3.
  3. The polynomials are equivalent.
