Review questions often combine exponent skills with earlier arithmetic.

Worked ExampleMissing Exponent
(5-6)(5n) = 535
  1. Use the product law: -6 + n = 35.
  2. Add 6 to both sides.
  3. n = 41.
Worked ExampleApplication
If 4x = 3, find 45x.
  1. 4⁵ˣ = (4ˣ)⁵.
  2. Substitute 4ˣ = 3.
  3. 3⁵ = 243.
