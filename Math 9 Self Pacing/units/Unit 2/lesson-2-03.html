Lesson 2.03

Product and Quotient with Coefficients

Apply the product and quotient laws while also multiplying or dividing coefficients.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Exponent Law Review

Product of Coefficients

Coefficients are multiplied normally while exponents on matching bases are added.

(3x2)(5x4)

3 × 5 = 15 and x² × x⁴ = x⁶.

Quotient of Coefficients

Coefficients are divided normally while exponents on matching bases are subtracted.

28b17 ÷ 4b15

28 ÷ 4 = 7 and b¹⁷ ÷ b¹⁵ = b².

New Learning

Coefficients with Exponent Laws

1

Handle coefficients separately.

Multiply or divide the numerical coefficients using ordinary arithmetic.

2

Apply exponent laws to each matching base.

Add exponents for products and subtract exponents for quotients.

3

Combine the simplified pieces.

Write the coefficient first, followed by each variable and its simplified exponent.

Worked ExampleProduct
(-4a5b2)(-5a2b6)
  1. Multiply coefficients: (-4)(-5) = 20.
  2. Add exponents on a: 5 + 2 = 7.
  3. Add exponents on b: 2 + 6 = 8.
  4. Answer: 20a⁷b⁸.
Worked ExampleQuotient
40d11 ÷ 8d7
  1. Divide coefficients: 40 ÷ 8 = 5.
  2. Subtract exponents: 11 - 7 = 4.
  3. Answer: 5d⁴.
