Product Law

Review powers and index notation, then use the product law to simplify products with the same base.

Practice Level Foundation
Quick Review

Powers and Index Notation

Parts of a Power

A power contains a base and an exponent. Algebraic terms may also have a coefficient.

2x3

2 is the coefficient, x is the base, and 3 is the exponent.

Repeated Multiplication

Exponential notation is a shorter way to write repeated multiplication.

7 × 7 × 7 = 73

The exponent tells how many times the base is used as a factor.

New Learning

Product Law of Exponents

1

Use the law only when the bases match.

The product law combines powers that have the same base.

2

Add the exponents.

For the same base: aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ.

3

Keep the base unchanged.

The base does not get multiplied by itself when applying the exponent law.

Worked ExampleSame Base
x4 × x3
  1. The bases are both x.
  2. Add the exponents: 4 + 3 = 7.
  3. Therefore, x⁴ × x³ = x⁷.
Worked ExampleNumeric Base
52 × 54
  1. The bases match.
  2. Add the exponents: 2 + 4 = 6.
  3. Therefore, 5² × 5⁴ = 5⁶.
